# Poya Haghnegahdar

**Physics PhD · Mathematical modelling · Applied AI**

I enjoy channelling the complexity and messiness of a problem into a structured, accessible stream of understanding—one that reveals the mechanics beneath the surface and opens a path to practical application. My work connects mathematical research, numerical experiments and software built around real decisions.

I began as an optimization researcher at **1QBit**, later becoming Head of Applications. I then co-founded **FIND Innovation Labs**, whose team developed and deployed ML software for supply-chain applications. Today, through **Build AI Technologies**, I explore practical applications of AI while continuing independent work on systematic trading research. I completed my **Physics PhD at UBC in May 2026**.

## Selected work

### Quantum computation: theory and numerical experiments
My doctoral research investigated computational resource states and measurement processes in measurement-based quantum computation. I combined mathematical proofs with Python Monte Carlo simulations that I designed and implemented, checking numerical results against analytical cases and examining convergence, sampling uncertainty and finite-size limitations.

[Read my thesis](https://hdl.handle.net/2429/93945)

### EdgeLoop: making trading decisions inspectable
An AI-assisted application for documenting trading frameworks, reviewing journal entries against stated rules, and analysing annotated charts. Its focus is helping traders examine the relationship between their intended process and their execution. My related research investigates whether explicitly defined strategies have a robust edge; that validation is ongoing.

[Visit EdgeLoop](https://edgeloop.trade)

### GroundTruth: property information into structured assessments
An applied AI project combining municipal planning rules, property location and land constraints to investigate development potential. The current work centres on zoning and entitlement lookup, source-linked information and spatial data. Broader feasibility analysis and automated monitoring are development goals.

### SnapTasks: natural-language task capture
An AI application that turns plain-language tasks into structured records with categories, priorities and due dates. It combines an LLM workflow with a persistent task-management interface.

[Visit SnapTasks](https://snaptasks.app)

### NLP and machine-learning experiments
Selected coursework and documented reproducibility extensions, including intent classification and statistical autocomplete. The public portfolio separates original learning projects from later AI-assisted revisions and records evaluation limitations.

[Explore the ML portfolio](https://github.com/PoyaH/poya-ml-portfolio)

## How I work

- Translate an ambiguous problem into explicit assumptions and testable questions.
- Build models and software, then examine their behaviour against appropriate reference cases.
- Make uncertainty and limitations visible alongside results.
- Connect research decisions with the needs of the people using the system.

My toolkit includes **Python, SQL, scientific computing, LLM APIs, TypeScript/Next.js, Supabase and Vercel**. I use AI-assisted development tools in my application work; my doctoral simulations were independently designed and implemented research software.
